\paragraph{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A precise account, with exhaustive checks rather than argument, of what a byte-level pre-transform layer contributes to a cipher (Section~\ref{sec:pretransform}).
  \item CPDPM, a context-bound AEAD construction: an injective context encoding, per-context HKDF keys under a versioned master key, key commitment, a single failure mode, a manifest that binds the set of encrypted fields in a FHIR resource, and a streaming mode; with a reduction to standard AEAD security (Section~\ref{sec:design}).
  \item An empirical demonstration that the construction rejects ciphertext substitution across patients, tenants and fields, and stale replay after key retirement, where shared-key and per-tenant baselines accept them; and a measurement of its cost against raw AES-GCM, Fernet and Tink (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}).
  \item An open, reproducible artifact: implementation, tests, known-answer vectors, and every experiment and figure.
\end{enumerate}
